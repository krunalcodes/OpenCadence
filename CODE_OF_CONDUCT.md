# Code of Conduct

OpenCadence follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Assume good intent, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any community channels.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact
details on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break these rules.
